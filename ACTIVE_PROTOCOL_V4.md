## Numerical support and scoring

Joint whitelist: current+port/starboard/bias/lag, port+bias, starboard+bias. All policies
can use all singles/pairs under equal budgets; policy-selected subsets differ. A pair
costs two parameter-start units/start. Sequential residual fitting ends in joint
optimization, not a claimed residual-only optimum. Complexity SSE+k log(5N), support
score improvement≥6, adequacy WRMS≤2, interior bounds, current references and declared
local-rank/correlation/residual checks are fixed in source before this run.

Private finite operational reference compares noiseless initial-window trajectories
against nominal or each one-component-removed alternative with declared five-channel
scales (.035,.004,.003,.05,.05); minimum normalized RMS>1 is distinguishable. It is a
finite known-parameter drop-component criterion under a Strict-empty active menu,
not global identifiability, a full registry search or physical irreducibility proof.
Out-of-registry/model-inadequate and nominal/control categories are separate. No label
uses policy success or is assigned after seeing model answers.

Primary: exact full physical cause set among preclassified distinguishable cases;
no missing/extra cause allowed. Nominal maps to an empty physical fault set, but a
scientific abstention is never counted as an exact nominal diagnosis. All-attempt
exactness/completion/failure outcomes are also reported. Partial intersections,
extra versus missing attribution, whole families, nonabstaining coverage and abstention
are separate. Correct/unnecessary abstention are conditional on the limited evaluator
criterion and never universal claims.

Parameter tolerance is separate and conditional on exact set plus known parameters:
current.1 m/s, efficiency.15, bias.08 rad, lag.2 s, dead zone.5 N, sway drag.3,
heading gain5 and lookahead2 (source table frozen before generation). Cause precision
and recall report their nonempty prediction/truth denominators; macro averages are
not calibrated confidence.

Statistics use physical cases, not telemetry rows or fits. Report Wilson95 intervals
for marginal proportions. Paired comparison reports discordants, exact two-sided
binomial/McNemar test and effect differences. A conservative95 effect interval uses
Bonferroni simultaneous97.5% Clopper-Pearson bounds on the two discordance probabilities.
It can be much wider than the conditional exact test suggests; overlapping Wilson
intervals are not used as a paired test. Balanced development strata are not deployment
prevalence; analyses are exploratory with no optional stopping based on policy wins.
