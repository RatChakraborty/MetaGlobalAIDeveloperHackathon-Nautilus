# Draft V3 protocol — not frozen

No V3 candidates have been generated. V1/V2 held-out pools are exposed historical
resources. Their results and original snapshots remain unchanged.

Before freezing, finalize the laboratory, public deployment uncertainty envelope,
runtime fallback, deterministic stopping rules and Muse implementation. Predeclare
separate development and held-out seed blocks after scanning every prior manifest
and source generator. No seed reservation in this draft constitutes a freeze.

Compare legacy passive inference and preserved historical policies separately
from the new shared-laboratory investigators: passive, exhaustive, fixed, random,
greedy and eventually Muse. Label inference changes and experiment-access changes
explicitly. To isolate investigator choice, use identical registry, methods,
observations, noise protocol, menu, resource budgets, safety screening, action caps
and stopping permissions. Do not grant Muse special tools.

Include all generated supported faults, healthy/mismatch controls, inadequate
models and cases with no admissible experiment. Rejections are outcomes, not
eligibility filters. Include both signs of sensor bias. Report incorrect confident
claims, correct labels, abstentions and reasons, model inadequacy/optimization
failures, attempted fits, wall time, logical starts, residual evaluations, parameter
errors by units, trials, simulated time, safety rejections, guard activations,
observed and evaluator-only true excursions, residual drift and fallback failures.

Use paired physical cases and descriptive Wilson intervals, with complete-pool
accounting and limited-prevalence qualifications. Numerical scores remain
uncalibrated. Freeze source/runtime fingerprints, distributions, seeds, budgets,
methods and all decision rules before generating the first held-out candidate.
Never tune on that pool. Retain wrong adequate fits and negative planner results.
